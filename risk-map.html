Across Myanmar / The hot season

How Heat Varies Across Myanmar

The map compares temperatures with local norms. Elevation, distance from the sea and the arrival of monsoon rain help explain why its patterns differ from place to place.

What lies behind the map

One season, distinct local patterns

Before the monsoon, strong sun and often-clear skies let heat build across Myanmar. The landscape shapes how long it lasts and how it feels.

  • Central dry zoneFar from the sea and sheltered from much of the rain, dry-zone towns can see intense afternoon heat and longer spells without rain.
  • Coast and deltaThese lowlands are generally more humid than the dry zone. As monsoon cloud and rain arrive, daytime temperatures can ease while humid nights may still feel warm.
  • UplandsHigher ground is generally cooler than the lowlands, though valleys and unusual hot spells can still bring uncomfortable heat.

As the monsoon arrives

Its onset is uneven, so cloud and rain often begin along the coast before reaching inland areas, so the central dry zone can stay hot between showers. More regular rain usually eases daytime temperatures, while humid air can keep nights warm.
