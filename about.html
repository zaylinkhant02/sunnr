Discover our purpose
01 / Our Identity

Who We Are

Sunnr is a student-led climate awareness project designed for Burmese communities. Our website aims to provide reliable information about heatwave risks while promoting sustainable solutions such as solar energy and climate adaptation strategies. Through education and technology, we seek to make climate information accessible, practical, and easy to understand.

02 / Our Purpose

Our Mission

Our mission is to create an informative and interactive platform that helps Burmese residents reduce the risks of climate-related heatwaves through heatwave monitoring, solar energy awareness, practical educational resources, and early warning alerts.

03 / Our Future

Our Vision

We envision a future where every community in Myanmar has access to accurate climate information, timely heatwave warnings, and sustainable technologies that improve resilience and protect people's health during extreme weather conditions.

Our Journey

Our Story

From recognising a growing challenge to building a practical digital resource.

Chapter 01

The Beginning

Sunnr was developed as part of a project focused on finding ways to reduce climate-related heatwave risks in Myanmar. As temperatures continue to rise, our team recognised the need for a platform that combines education, monitoring, and practical solutions.

Chapter 02

Research and Learning

We investigated the causes and impacts of heatwaves, studying scientific reports, climate data, and real-world challenges faced by communities affected by extreme heat.

Chapter 03

Building Sunnr

Using our research, we designed Sunnr to provide heatwave monitoring, solar energy information, informative resources, and early warning alerts in one easy-to-use platform for Burmese residents.

Chapter 04

Looking Ahead

We hope Sunnr will continue to raise climate awareness, encourage sustainable living, and help communities become more prepared for future heatwave events.
